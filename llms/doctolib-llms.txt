# Doctolib

> Doctolib is a European e-health company (founded 2013, headquartered in Levallois-Perret, France) providing an online medical appointment-booking platform and practice-management software for healthcare professionals and patients across France, Germany, Italy and the Netherlands. Its products include appointment booking, telehealth, patient messaging, clinical software, patient billing and the Doctolib Connect integration/SSO surface. Doctolib exposes a partner/integration API through an authentication-gated developer portal; no public OpenAPI, SDK, or hosted MCP server is published.
